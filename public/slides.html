The problem

Heat deaths happen quietly, indoors, to people alone.

619

people died in British Columbia's 2021 heat dome. 98% died indoors.

56%

of them lived alone. Two-thirds were 70 or older.

70%

of A/C units weren't working where people died of heat indoors in Phoenix's Maricopa County, 2024.

Who it's for: neighborhood mutual-aid groups, senior centers and churches, where one volunteer coordinator juggles a spreadsheet, a phone and a heat warning. B.C.'s coroners recommended prioritizing people who live alone for home visits during extreme heat.
Sources: BC Coroners Service, Extreme Heat and Human Mortality: A Review of Heat-Related Deaths in B.C. in Summer 2021 (2022). Maricopa County Department of Public Health, 2024 heat deaths report: 602 confirmed deaths; 88% of indoor deaths had an A/C unit present, 70% of those units non-functional.
Our solution · how it works

The agent does the legwork. The coordinator makes the calls.

01 · WATCH

Heat warning

Live National Weather Service alerts via check_heat_alert.

02 · TRIAGE

Score & route

Fixed risk rules; nearest volunteer, highest risk first.

03 · FOLLOW UP

Read reports

The model reads volunteers' notes; rules book cooling rides and retries.

04 · ESCALATE

Only when it matters

Two unanswered visits → one decision card for the human.

05 · CLOSE THE LOOP

Act on approval

Welfare check or second visit, then track until reached.
