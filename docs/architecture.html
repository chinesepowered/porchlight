Guardrail hook · BeforeToolCallEvent
  • No scoring, routing or texting without an active heat warning
  • Max 2 automated texts per resident per hour; max 14 tool calls per run
  • Escalation only after two unanswered visits; no duplicate cards
  • Welfare check / entry only with coordinator approval, carried out before anything else
Deterministic rules · lib/rules.ts

Risk score (age, lives alone, AC, heat-sensitive meds, mobility, top floor) · greedy nearest-volunteer assignment with capacity, high-risk stops first · nearest cooling center · reach counts.
